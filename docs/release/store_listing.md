## English

**Title:** SpiritWorld City

**Short description (≤ 80):** Care for your city as a pastor – in conversation, prayer and the invisible world.

**App Store subtitle (≤ 30):** Prayer changes the city

**Description:**
You are a pastor in a city that needs light.

💬 **Meet people** – Everyone has their own life story. Listen, talk, pray with them and walk step by step through what shaped them.

🙏 **Two worlds** – Switch to the invisible world, where darkness swirls over the city. In prayer, demons give way and light spreads.

🏘️ **Awaken neighbourhoods** – Once enough people in a neighbourhood have found faith, the stronghold of darkness opens. Through persistent prayer it falls – and the neighbourhood awakens.

⛪ **Build a church** – New believers join the church as intercessors, small-group leaders and helpers – and those who have been healed help others with the same wound.

🎓 **Learn** – Mentors teach you pastoral care, spiritual warfare, discernment, intercession, fasting and preaching – each with a Bible verse.

✨ Almost no text – the game tells its story with symbols, great for kids and families.
🔒 No ads, no purchases, no data collection. Saves stay on your device.

**Category:** Games → Simulation (alt. Adventure)
**Keywords:** christian, prayer, bible, church, family, pixel, city, simulation
